⚠️ Notes & decisions

Two real BLOCKERs caught across two review passes — not stylistic nitpicks. First: an Upcoming-row edit could bypass approval entirely. Second: the toggle-off install-neutrality promise wasn't actually enforced at the service layer, only at the UI/trigger layer — a privileged or direct caller could run the whole pipeline while the feature was supposedly inactive. Both are exactly the class of bug that's invisible until someone (or some integration) calls the Apex directly.
Dispositioned, not applied: a reviewer flagged the ohfy__ prefix in PriceApproval_TestData.finishedGoodInFlpLine as a Hard-Constraint-7 violation. Author's counter: TestDataFactory.createSObject resolves its string args against the running org's global describe at runtime — in the namespaced CI org those keys ARE ohfy__-prefixed, so dropping the prefix breaks there. Rule 7 targets compile-time SOQL/Schema refs, not this runtime-resolved factory arg. Root CLAUDE.md's own Testing Convention example prescribes the prefix for createSObject. Verified, not just asserted.
Zero price-resolver risk by construction. Pending prices stay in Draft status the whole time they're in the approval chain — the price resolver already excludes Draft rows, so this feature cannot affect what price a customer sees today, toggle on or off. That's a stronger safety property than most feature flags get.
Explicitly out of scope (BMS-4133, Phase 2): delegation / out-of-office coverage, per-line exclusion from a batch, a full audit-trail timeline view, emergency override. Rejection is terminal in Ph 1 — no re-open, only resubmit-as-new-request.
