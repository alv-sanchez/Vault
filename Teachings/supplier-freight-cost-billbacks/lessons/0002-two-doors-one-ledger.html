Lesson 2 · Epic BMS-5161 — Supplier Freight Cost Billbacks

Two doors, one ledger

Now the mechanism. Freight enters through two doors; both walk the same hallway into the same billback ledger. Learn the hallway once and you know both paths.

The two doors (where freight enters)

DoorObjectWhat arms itStory
Door 1 — TransferTransfer_Group__cStatus goes to Complete (cross-state, freight + account set)BMS-5789 / 5790
Door 2 — POPurchase_Order__cSupplier_Owes_Freight__c checked at reconciliation (+ amount + supplier)BMS-5791

Why two different triggers? Freight on a transfer is known when the move completes. Freight on a PO is discovered when finance reconciles it — a separate act from the PO closing. Same outcome, different real-world moment.

The hallway (what both doors walk)

GATE
The trigger on the source object checks eligibility (right transition / freight-owed signal).TransferGroupTriggerService · PurchaseOrderTriggerService — afterUpdate
RESOLVE
Resolve the billback service through the Tier-1 Service-Locator interface — WMS never calls OMS directly.ServiceLocator.resolve('FreightBillbackService' / 'POFreightBillbackService')
ENQUEUE
Hand off to an async queueable so the user's save returns instantly.Q_FreightBillbackGeneration · Q_POFreightBillbackGeneration
CALCULATE
Pull the supplier's coverage %, compute recoverable, idempotently upsert the Billback + line (keyed by deterministic External_Id).S_FreightBillbackCalculation · S_POFreightBillbackCalculation → S_SupplierFundingAgreement
The destination A Billback__c with Type = Freight (+ a Billback_Line__c pointing back at the source). It lands in the same BMS-4141 ledger as rebates, co-op, MDF. Freight is a value in the Type__c picklist — not a new object.

The money, worked

$1,200 cross-state freight, supplier funding agreement at 40% → $480 recoverable. $300 PO freight at 40% → $120. The coverage % cascades: agreement → account default → capped at 100%.

Two guardrails you built in

  • Field-lock after Accrued — once a freight billback is Invoiced/Collected/Written-Off, the source freight fields go read-only (UI and server) so a transfer can't drift from an already-billed amount.
  • Freight-vs-claim overlap audit — a nightly check stamps Has_Related_Claim__c when the same shipment also has a damage claim, so Gulf reviews before double-recovering (freight = transportation cost only).
A PO already has Supplier_Owes_Freight__c = true. Someone flips its Status to Complete but touches no freight field. Does a billback generate?
