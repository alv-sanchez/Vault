### Residual scope — v1 acceptance criteria

Per the Jira Scope Decisions (2026-06-11): direct-write + notification, no approval process, no zone model, no external address validation, no `Account_Change_Request__c`.

1. **Delivery address display** — ecomProfilePage shows the Account's delivery (Shipping*) address — street, city, state, ZIP, country — in a dedicated "Delivery Address" card. (`Shipping_Address__c` is a formula over the standard Shipping fields → standard fields are the source of truth; evidence: `OHFY-Data-Model/.../Account/fields/Shipping_Address__c.field-meta.xml`.)
2. **Delivery address edit (direct-write)** — logged-in retailer edits ShippingStreet/City/State/PostalCode/Country inline; street/city/state/ZIP required, ZIP must be 5 digits; save writes directly to Account (no approval); success toast; card reflects the new address.
3. **Address-change notification** — on successful save, the account's Sales Rep (`Account.Sales_Rep__c` user) receives an email summarizing old → new address; notification failure never rolls back the address save (fire-and-forget).
4. **Contacts list** — ecomProfilePage shows all contacts on the retailer's account (name, email, phone, title) in an "Account Contacts" card.
5. **Add contact** — retailer adds a contact (first/last/email required; phone, title optional) to their own account; duplicate email on the same account is blocked with an error naming the conflict; new contact appears in the list without reload.
6. **Edit contact** — retailer edits first/last/phone/title of any contact on their own account (email immutable post-create, consistent with the shipped own-contact edit); server rejects edits to contacts outside the caller's account.
7. **Server-side authorization** — every new Apex write derives the target Account from `UserInfo.getUserId() → User.ContactId → Contact.AccountId`; client-supplied account ids are never trusted for writes.
8. **Permissions committed (Gate 2c)** — "Ohanafy Community User" profile gains `apexClassAccesses` for the new controller and Contact object create/edit; works on a setup-site.sh-only org.
9. **Tests** — `AccountManagementController_T` (assertions on every method incl. authorization-failure paths), `Ecom_UI_Wrappers_T` delegation, Playwright `tests/ecom/account-management.spec.ts`.
