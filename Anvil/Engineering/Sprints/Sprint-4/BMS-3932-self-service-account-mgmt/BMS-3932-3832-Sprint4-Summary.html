BMS-3832

CTC Suggestion Engine

Backlog Story Inventory Gulf Needs Spike

Impact

Gulf distributes perishable beverages across 5 warehouses. Close-to-code inventory that isn't moved quickly becomes a direct P&L hit through spoilage write-offs, supplier claim rejections, and customer returns.

Warehouses
5 locations (FL + AL)
SKU Complexity
Thousands, varying shelf-life
Pricing Codes
154+ (promotional levers)
P&L Risk
Spoilage, failed claims, returns

Solution

  1. Scan aging inventory Scheduled batch queries Lot__c.Expiration_Date__c against configurable CTC thresholds per brand/warehouse. Lot tracking infrastructure already exists.
  2. Calculate route velocity Uses existing Placement__c.Weekly_Sales__c (rate-of-sale at Account+Item level) and Invoice_Item__c history to match aging inventory to high-velocity routes.
  3. Generate CTC suggestions New CTC_Suggestion__c records with warehouse, SKU, quantity, days-until-code, priority, and suggested action (Route Push / Discount / Transfer / Supplier Return).
  4. Interwarehouse transfer recommendations Leverages existing Transfer__c + Transfer_Group__c objects. Accounts for transit time between warehouses.
  5. Pricing-aware suggestions References promotional Price_Record__c pricing. Calculates margin impact vs. spoilage write-off cost.
  6. Manager dashboard LWC for warehouse managers to Accept/Reject suggestions with audit trail and configurable reason codes (CMDT).

Connection to Real-Time Inventory Reconciliation (BMS-3825)

Both BMS-3832 (CTC Engine) and BMS-3825 (Inventory Reconciliation) operate on the same inventory data layer but address different problems. The CTC engine reads lot-level expiration data and sell-through velocity; reconciliation ensures the underlying quantities are accurate.

Dimension BMS-3825 (Reconciliation) BMS-3832 (CTC Engine)
Problem Counted quantities don't match system during concurrent operations Aging inventory sits in warehouses until it expires

Reads from Current inventory state (point-in-time freeze) Lot expiration dates + historical sell-through velocity

Key dependency: BMS-3832 requires reliable lot-level quantities. If reconciliation (BMS-3825) reveals that Lot_Inventory__c.Quantity_On_Hand__c is inaccurate, CTC suggestions will inherit that inaccuracy. Not a formal blocker, but accurate inventory is a quality prerequisite.
