Supplier Freight Cost Billbacks

Gulf pays freight that suppliers are supposed to pay. There was no way to get that money back — now there is.

Stop absorbing freight suppliers owe → capture it → bill it back

What we're solving (say this)

Three sentences — the whole story:

  1. When Gulf ships product — across state lines, or inbound from a supplier — it often pays the freight, but by contract the supplier owes it.
  2. Before, that cost just sat on Gulf's books and got absorbed — money quietly lost.
  3. Now we capture the freight where it happens and automatically bill it back to the supplier, at the coverage % from their funding agreement.
The smart part: we didn't build a new recovery system. Gulf already had a billback ledger (rebates, co-op, MDF). Freight is just a new type of billback fed by two new sources. One ledger, two doors.

Live demo script

  1. Frame it — "Gulf was eating freight that suppliers owe. Watch us capture it and bill it back."
  2. Door 1 — Transfer: open a cross-state transfer, click Enter Freight Cost, enter the cost + supplier, Save, then set Status → Complete. The Supplier Freight Billback panel shows the recovered amount at the agreement's coverage %.
  3. Door 2 — PO: open a supplier PO, check Supplier Owes Freight with a recoverable amount → the same freight billback appears from the PO side.
  4. Land it — "Same billback rails Gulf already uses for rebates. Freight's just a new source. Real dollars back, fully audited, and it won't double-recover against a claim."
If you blank: "We stop Gulf from eating freight suppliers owe — we capture it and bill it back automatically." + the two numbers ($480, $120).
