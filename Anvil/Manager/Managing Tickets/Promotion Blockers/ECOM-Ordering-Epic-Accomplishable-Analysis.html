1How this backlog was built — the context behind the tickets

Reading the not-Done pool correctly requires knowing where it came from. This epic's tickets were not hand-authored one by one — they were generated and enriched from workshop notes + codebase analysis, then decomposed from umbrella stories. That origin is exactly why the pool carries duplicate umbrellas and auto-generated "Needs Refinement" stubs on top of the real phased build.

Why this matters for the count. The raw "~13 open" overstates real work because the backlog still carries (a) auto-generated umbrella stories that duplicate the phased children (3924/3925/3928), and (b) enrichment scaffolding — generated Approaches/ACs marked "verify before implementation." Strip those and the genuine remaining build is small. The classification in §2 is the result of verifying each generated ticket against what's actually in the repo.
Trust level on the source data. Workshop context and pain points are high-signal (real customer sessions). The auto-generated Approach / AC / Readiness fields are advisory — they're a starting point from a codebase-similarity pass, not a verified design, and the tickets say so explicitly. This analysis leans on the workshop context + live-repo verification, and treats the generated approach text as a hint only.
