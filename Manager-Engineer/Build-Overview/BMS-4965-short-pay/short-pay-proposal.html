💸 Short Pay Automation — Proposal & Feedback

BMS-4965 · AR-Finance / Delivery · owner Alvaro Sanchez · PO Elliot Flores · we need your input on 5 decisions (below)

🧠 In one line: When a Gulf driver collects less than the invoice at delivery, today it's a manual, error-prone chase. This captures the short pay at the truck, gates big ones behind an approval, escalates by each state's rules, and resolves it to a credit or a collection — so leaked cash gets recovered and every short pay is auditable.

👤 Who it's for

Gulf delivery drivers (capture at the stop) and the AR / finance team (back-office resolution).

💥 Why it matters

Short pays happen regularly; the gap is real money. Today there's no back-office flow — collections/credits get missed and there's no FL/AL compliance trail. This recovers cash at the source with an audit trail.

🛠️ The approach

Extend the shipped capture (no new objects): driver logs the short pay → configurable approval gate on big ones → back-office queue → auto-escalation by state → resolve to credit or collected.

🚀 What's next

Land the driver approval gate (in review), then plug in the FL/AL threshold numbers + confirm the EFT and AR-aging phases (need your calls below).
