New here? Shift-end / warehouse ops primer

New to warehouse operations and to Salesforce? Read this first. It explains, in plain language, every idea this epic touches. Everything below assumes you have read it.

What is a "shift"?

A defined working period in the warehouse — Day, Night, or Mid. Gulf runs day/night shifts across all 5 warehouses; Milton FL also has a mid-shift. Each shift is a different crew supervised by a different person. At shift end, the outgoing supervisor must hand off the warehouse to the incoming crew.

What is "breakage"?

Product damaged during a shift — dropped cases, spills, damaged-on-arrival product discovered during picking. In beverage distribution, breakage is routine and must be counted so it can be subtracted from inventory and reported to finance as shrinkage.

What is "shrinkage"?

The total unexplained loss of inventory. Breakage is the most common source. Without a formal checklist, breakage quietly becomes invisible shrinkage — product that vanished but nobody can explain when, who, or which shift caused it.

What is a pick event?

A warehouse scan event (Pick_Event__c) recorded when a picker completes a pick or short-picks. Breakage events have Reason__c = DAMAGED and accumulate throughout the shift — they are the raw data source for the shift-end breakage review.

What is a "pass-down note"?

A written note from the outgoing supervisor to the incoming crew — "Bay 7 has a cracked floor tile," "Modelo restock delayed until 6AM." Today these are verbal or skipped. The shift-end checklist provides a structured place to record them (Shift_Pass_Down__c).

Why "back-office is Phase 3"?

Phase 1 (this build) focuses on the supervisor's screen: see breakage, work through checklist items, leave notes. The finance-facing pieces — a single Total_Breakage_Cases__c rollup on the checklist header and the submission flow that triggers it — are Phase 3 (BMS-4080), not yet built. The rollup field reads 0 in Phase 1.

What is the checklist screen?

A single Salesforce LWC screen the supervisor opens at shift close. It loads from ShiftEndChecklistController.loadChecklist(warehouseId, shift, shiftDate) and returns a bundle: the checklist record, checklist items, damaged events (breakage), and pass-down notes — everything the supervisor needs to close the shift.

What is the Total_Breakage_Cases__c gap?

The header field Shift_End_Checklist__c.Total_Breakage_Cases__c reads 0 in Phase 1 — the rollup that populates it is BMS-4080 (not yet built). The screen shows the correct total by summing Pick_Event__c.Breakage_Quantity__c at runtime. This is a known Phase-1 gap, not a bug. Mention it proactively when demoing.

A worked example

A Milton FL mid-shift runs 11 PM–7 AM. Three pickers each damage some cases during picks. Those events land in Pick_Event__c as the shift runs. At 6:45 AM the supervisor opens the checklist:

Breakage review: 9 events   →   34 total damaged cases
→ supervisor reviews checklist items (Safety ✓, Equipment ✓, Replenishment ✗, Other ✗) → leaves 2 pass-down notes → saves. Incoming day-shift supervisor opens the record and sees exactly what happened.

Go deeper — the shift-end checklist lifecycle (Phase 1 → 3)

One shift travels through these stages. Stages 1–2 are what Phase 1 builds; stages 3–4 are future phases.

  1. Breakage accumulates during the shift data already exists
    Pick_Event__c records with Reason__c = DAMAGED and Breakage_Quantity__c set accumulate throughout the shift. These are created by the picking flow (already shipped). The checklist reads them — it does not write them.
  2. Supervisor opens the checklist (Phase 1 — this build) built · BMS-4078
    ShiftEndChecklistController.loadChecklist(warehouseId, shift, shiftDate) returns a bundle: the checklist record (created on first open via S_ShiftEndChecklist.initiate()), checklist items (Safety/Equipment/Replenishment/Other), damaged events, and pass-down notes. Supervisor reviews breakage, checks off items, adds notes. Known gap: Total_Breakage_Cases__c reads 0 — rollup is Phase 3.
  3. Cycle counts + structured pass-down (Phase 2 — BMS-4079)
    Formal cycle counts tied to the shift. A structured pass-down entry UI so incoming supervisors can filter by category or flag. Still seeded as free-text Shift_Pass_Down__c in Phase 1.
  4. Submit + finance reporting (Phase 3 — BMS-4080)
    Supervisor submits the checklist. The Total_Breakage_Cases__c rollup fires, producing a single shrinkage number tied to the shift for finance reporting. The checklist status transitions from In Progress → Submitted.
Go deeper — the Total_Breakage_Cases__c = 0 gap (Phase 1 known limitation)

Shift_End_Checklist__c.Total_Breakage_Cases__c is the headline shrinkage field on the checklist record. In Phase 1 it reads 0. Here is why, and what to say when demoing:

  • Why it reads 0: The rollup that populates it (summing Breakage_Quantity__c across the shift's damaged Pick_Event__c records onto the checklist header) is ticket BMS-4080 — Phase 3. It is not yet built. Without the rollup trigger, the field sits at its default (0).
  • What the screen actually shows: S_BreakageReview.getDamagedEvents() returns all damaged events for the GMT day window. The LWC sums Breakage_Quantity__c across those events at render time, showing the correct total (34 cases in the demo). The real number is correct on screen — it just doesn't live in a Salesforce field yet.
  • What to say when demoing: "The screen pulls the breakage total from the live events list — you can see 34 cases here. The rollup to the checklist record header is Phase 3; in the demo that field shows 0, which is expected. The full Phase 3 build will write that total to the record so finance reporting can use it directly."
  • This is not a bug: Phase 1 scope is the checklist screen and breakage review. The rollup is deliberately deferred to Phase 3 to keep Phase 1 focused and shippable.
Go deeper — why new objects instead of reusing existing WMS records

The shift-end checklist is a new, distinct concept that doesn't map cleanly onto any existing WMS object:

  • Shift-level, not pick-level. A checklist is a supervisor-level record for a whole shift. Pick_Event__c records are individual scan events — too granular to carry checklist state or pass-down notes.
  • Checklist items are independent. Safety / Equipment / Replenishment / Other are not pick-event categories — they're supervisor judgment checks. A separate Shift_End_Checklist_Item__c child lets them be added, removed, and checked off independently.
  • Pass-down notes have their own lifecycle. Shift_Pass_Down__c is authored by one supervisor and consumed by the next. It needs its own fields (Category__c, Flagged_For_Next_Shift__c, Author__c) that don't exist on any current object.
  • The breakage data already exists. Rather than duplicating breakage counts, the checklist reads Pick_Event__c damaged events live. Zero duplication of pick-layer data.

🧱 What's being built

  • Shift_End_Checklist__c — (Warehouse__c, Shift__c, Shift_Date__c, Status__c, Total_Breakage_Cases__c). Status: In Progress → Submitted (Ph3). Keyed by (warehouse, shift, date).
  • Shift_End_Checklist_Item__c — child checklist items (Item_Type__c: Safety / Equipment / Replenishment / Other; Notes__c; Is_Complete__c).
  • Shift_Pass_Down__c — pass-down notes from outgoing to incoming crew (Category__c, Note__c, Flagged_For_Next_Shift__c, Author__c).
  • ShiftEndChecklistController — LWC Apex controller: loadChecklist(warehouseId, shift, shiftDate) returns the full bundle.
  • S_ShiftEndChecklist — checklist service: initiate() creates or retrieves the checklist record for a given (warehouse, shift, date).
  • S_BreakageReview — breakage service: getDamagedEvents() returns damaged Pick_Event__c records for the GMT day window.
  • LWC shiftEndChecklist — the single-page supervisor screen in OHFY-WMS-UI.
  • Demo_Shift_End_Tab permission set — grants tab visibility for the demo org.
