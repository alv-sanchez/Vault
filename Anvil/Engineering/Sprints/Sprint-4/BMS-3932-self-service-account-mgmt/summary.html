BMS-3825

Real-Time Inventory Reconciliation

In Progress Story Gulf Not Ready to Pick Up

Summary

Gulf's warehouses run continuous operations — drivers loading, receiving putting away, transfers in-flight. When they do a cycle count or full physical inventory, live quantities keep changing underneath the count, making results unreliable the moment counting starts.

Gulf currently relies on Vermont Information Processing for manual keying of discrepancies — painful and error-prone. They need a point-in-time snapshot mechanism so counting doesn't require freezing warehouse operations, and Finance can reconcile snapshot-vs-actual variances after counting completes.

GL/chart-of-accounts adjustments must not auto-trigger until variances are formally reviewed and approved.

Proposed Solution

  1. Point-in-time snapshot Warehouse manager initiates a cycle count or full physical inventory. System freezes a snapshot of current quantities at that moment.
  2. Operations continue uninterrupted Receiving, picking, loading, and transfers keep running. Operational deltas are tracked against the snapshot baseline.
  3. Count entry Warehouse staff enters actual counted quantities against the snapshot baseline.
  4. Variance reconciliation Finance reviews snapshot-vs-actual variances. GL adjustments only apply after formal approval — never auto-triggered.
