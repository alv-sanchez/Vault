Pricing precedence (summary — full tables in front-line-pricing.md)

Your Price = FLP base − winning-promotion discount. FLP picks the most specific row (ACCOUNT_ITEM → ITEM → ITEM_TYPE_PKG → ITEM_LINE_PKG → ITEM_DEFAULT → NONE). Promotions then pick one winner per line: scope-match → Active+in-window → QD highest tier ≤ qty → priority-winnow → deepest per-case discount wins (no stacking in v1). Label: tier → "Volume Price", flat → "Promo Price".
