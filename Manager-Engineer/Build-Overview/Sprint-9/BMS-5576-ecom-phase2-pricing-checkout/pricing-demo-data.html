Resolution rules (S_PriceResolver)

#Rule
1Base: most-specific matching Front_Line_Price__c for the shopper's pricing group; else the item's default case price.
2Eligibility: promotion must be Active, in its date window, and in the shopper's audience (promotion group, chain, channel, warehouse, or direct Account__c target).
3One winner per line: when several promos qualify, the deepest discount wins — promotions never stack.
4QD tiers use the whole-cart quantity for that item — the deepest tier reached applies to every case on the line.
5Labels: flat/Post-off → Promo Price · tier reached → Volume Price, always with the struck base beside the final price.
6Same engine everywhere: shop card, product page, cart, review summary, and the confirmed invoice all call the same resolver — the number can't disagree across pages.
