## 1. Overview

**Purpose:** Account settings page where retailers view and update their contact information, business details, and notification preferences.

**Target Users:** Retailers

---

## 2. Features

- View/edit contact name, phone, email
- View business info: sales rep, fulfillment location, pricelist, territory
- View billing address
- Notification preferences (via `NotificationPreferenceController`)
