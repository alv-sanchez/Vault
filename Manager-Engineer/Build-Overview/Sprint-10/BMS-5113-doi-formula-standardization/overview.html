👤 User

Purchasing/warehouse planners across Gulf's 5 warehouses who use Days-on-Inventory to decide when to reorder or flag overstock.

💥 Business impact

Inconsistent DOI formulas across departments produced conflicting signals — one warehouse sees a SKU overstocked, another sees it critically low — causing duplicated POs and missed replenishment.

🛠️ Intended solution

Extends the shipped S_InventoryDOI/B_InventoryDOI (BMS-3779) — swaps the sales-rate source to completed invoiced quantity, adds 30/60/90-day windows.

🚀 Next phase

Live before/after DOI reconciliation against real data + PO sign-off before BMS-3742 goes ready-for-review.

What it is

A single, authoritative DOI calculation, computed once and read everywhere — replacing a distorted on-hand-decrease proxy with the BMS-3817 spike's specified source: completed invoiced quantity.

Status / what's built

  • BMS-4543/4544/4545 — not started; largely pre-answered/pre-delivered per code audit
