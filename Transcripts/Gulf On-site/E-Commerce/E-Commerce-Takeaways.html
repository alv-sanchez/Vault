What Gulf wants from the B2B storefront — and where to swing first

Source: Gulf on-site, Jul 15 (Sales Day 1, 3h 11m) Owner: Alvaro Sanchez (E-Comm SME) Prepared: 2026-07-21

The storefront is ~11–12% of revenue, yet ~75% of those accounts never see a rep and Gulf has zero visibility into how they behave online. Olivia's framing: "this has got to be our salesperson." The mandate is to turn an order-pad into a selling engine — and to light up the operational blind spots first.

Biggest friction points — ranked by relief-per-effort

Flying blind on the channel that is 12% of revenue
No metrics on who's logging in, who orders on time, who's gone quiet, or where carts die. Suppliers ask "what did you sell online" and there's no answer.
Alleviate: ship short-term ops metrics now — signups, on-time-order %, dormant 30/60/90-day accounts → rep/GM/SVP dashboards. Full web-funnel analytics follow.

🚩 The one dependency that can't be lost
The whole suggestive-selling vision rests on Ohanafy's promise that items, pricing, promotions & MBOs surface automatically by eligibility (account + start/end date) from the core system — the storefront is just a lens. But MBOs / promos / incentives are not structured records today — they live in spreadsheets, special-events sheets, other people's budgets, and "Jimmy's head." Olivia herself "doesn't do the MBOs or track incentives." Building a promotion object with eligibility + dates is upstream of E-Comm (a sales/marketing/budgeting build). Track it as DEP-1 so a missing prerequisite doesn't read later as "e-comm failed to deliver upsell."

What Gulf explicitly wants (their words)

A selling engine, not an order pad.
"Our E-commerce platform should be pushing that agenda for us… it should be suggestive selling that to them until they add it to cart and check out."
Visibility into online behavior.
"We have zero line of sight of how our customers are behaving online… this has got to be our salesperson."
Self-service & less manual work.
Self-registration, modify-your-own-order, auto-enable new accounts — stop routing everything through one person.
Suppliers own their content.
A supplier portal where suppliers upload images/assets at new-item intake — "I want them to own it."
Flexibility that keeps up with the calendar.
MBOs change monthly, drives end mid-month — surfacing must be eligibility + date-driven, self-maintaining.

Alignments to hold onto

  • Storefront = a lens into core. Items/pricing/promos/MBOs are managed in the core env and surface by eligibility — no separate e-comm backend to maintain. Every "can it do X" rests on this.
  • Resale/POS is out of the storefront for now — decided live; keep with the sales team, turn on later. Signage requests are wanted.
  • Mobile app is uncommitted — Gulf assumes app + website; flag as roadmap.
  • Full marketing-style analytics are roadmap, not near-term — set expectations; deliver ops metrics first.
  • Order confirmation ≠ notification — treat separately for SMS opt-in / STOP compliance.
