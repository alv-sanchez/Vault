👤 User

The Gulf launch coordinator who owns getting a new product to market — responsible for pulling purchasing, warehouse, marketing, and sales into one plan.

💥 Business impact

New-product launches (~16+/yr at Gulf) run on scattered spreadsheets and memory today. No shared view means the first sign of trouble is the worst one: a promo fires with no product on shelf, or POS materials land weeks late.

🛠️ Intended solution

A Product_Launch__c record per product with an auto-generated 4-lane checklist, each step owned + due-dated backward from launch day, rolling up to per-lane ready flags and an overall readiness %. Tracking-only — no activation gating.

🚀 Next phase

Two not-yet-groomed REQ-009 safeguards (supply-in-position gate, promo-timing guard) will read these readiness fields. Both explicitly out of scope here.

The four lanes

Purchasing

Confirm supplier & PO (–45d) → PO received into DC (–7d)

Warehouse Readiness

Slotting & location setup (–21d) → Inventory staged at DCs (–3d)

POS Material

Design POS materials (–30d) → Delivered to accounts (–5d)

Sales Activation

Pricelist & promo configured (–14d) → Sales team briefed (–2d)

Offsets are days-before-launch, from the curated default template in S_ProductLaunch.TEMPLATE — not derived from allocation size or per-warehouse scoping (out of scope, see open questions).

Where each lane's context signal comes from

Three of the four lanes show a read-only, informational line above their steps (e.g. "Inventory staged at 6 location(s)", "No open purchase orders"). These are live queries in ProductLaunchController.buildContextSignals(itemId) against the launch's Item__c — they never drive the ready flags, which come only from step completion.

LaneSource objectQuery
PurchasingPurchase_Order_Item__cItem__c = :itemId AND Outstanding_Case_Quantity__c > 0 — count of open PO lines + sum of outstanding cases.
Warehouse ReadinessInventory__cItem__c = :itemId — distinct count of Location__c the item has inventory staged at.
Sales ActivationPromotion__cItem__c = :itemId, ordered by Start_Date__c — count of scheduled promotions + earliest start date.
POS MaterialnoneNo context signal wired — this lane shows only its steps, no summary line. Gap, not yet flagged as an open question.

Validated behavior (live, on ohfy-bms5592)

  • Creating a launch with an Item__c + Launch_Date__c auto-generates 8 steps across 4 lanes; owner defaults to the creator (placeholder, reassignable); due date = launch − offset.
  • A step whose due date already fell in the past is flagged At_Risk__c immediately on creation.
  • Completing every step in a lane flips that lane's ready flag; Readiness_Percent__c climbs (Purchasing-only-complete → 25%).
  • Moving the launch date recalculates every step's due date and at-risk flag (3 steps flip at-risk when moved to 5 days out).
  • Inserting with no Launch_Date__c is blocked (REQUIRED_FIELD_MISSING) — no partial record created.
  • Status can only move between Not Started / In Progress / Complete / Blocked (restricted picklist).

Demo script

Add the Product Launch Plan LWC to a Product_Launch__c record page (or use the new default FlexiPage once committed). Create a launch with an Item + Launch Date → four lanes appear, each with a ready badge and a read-only context signal (Inventory/PO/Promotion). Flip a step to Complete → its lane badge + readiness % update live. Reassign an owner inline. Change the launch date → every due date and at-risk flag recalculates.

Don't say this gates anything — it's tracking-only. The Inventory/PO/Promotion strings are informational context; the ready flags come from step completion, not from those signals.
