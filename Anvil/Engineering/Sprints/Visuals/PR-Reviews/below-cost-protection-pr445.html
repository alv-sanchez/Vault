The Problem

Nothing stops a line from selling under cost

A forward-sale invoice line's final price is the product of several independent systems layered on top of each other — front-line pricing, promotions, one-day sales, manual overrides, even offline truck sales synced later. None of those systems talk to each other, and none of them check the result against what the item actually costs to land (Item.Last_Landed_Case_Cost__c).

  • Silent margin erosion — a 10% promo stacked on a 15% route discount can push a case under cost with nobody noticing until finance reconciles at month-end
  • Supplier rebate exposure — some supplier agreements condition rebate eligibility on maintaining a price floor; a below-cost sale can jeopardize that without anyone knowing it happened
  • No pipeline-agnostic check existed — whatever guardrails existed lived inside individual pricing paths, so a below-cost price from a path nobody thought to check (e.g. a manual override on an existing line) sailed through untouched
The Solution

One check, after everything else has already priced the line

Below-Cost Protection doesn't care how a line got its price. It reads the line's net case price — whatever survived front-line pricing, promotions, and manual edits — and compares it to the item's cost floor at save time, on every insert and update.

  • Pipeline-agnostic — front-line pricing, promotions, one-day sales, manual overrides, and the future full-waterfall resolver (BMS-3874) all get caught the same way, because the check runs after the price is already decided
  • Two new fields, always populated — Is_Below_Cost__c (checkbox) and Below_Cost_Variance__c (currency, negative = under cost, per case)
  • Two independent levers — the advisory flag and the enforcement block are separate trigger methods with their own bypass switches, so one can be turned off for a data load without silently disabling the other
  • Ships inert — blocking is off by default; every org gets the flag as pure data first, and an admin opts into enforcement after reviewing what gets caught
How a Line Gets Evaluated
1

Price Lands

Front-line pricing, a promotion, a manual edit, or an offline truck sale sets Case_Price__c and Total_Discount_Percent__c on the line

→
2

Net Price

Case_Price__c × (1 − Total_Discount_Percent__c ÷ 100) — the price the customer actually pays per case

→
3

Cost Floor

Item.Last_Landed_Case_Cost__c — the most recent landed cost per case from the PO receipt pipeline

→
4

Flag

Net < floor → Is_Below_Cost__c = true, Below_Cost_Variance__c = net − floor (always runs)

→
5

Block (if active)

Commit to a live invoice, or submit a draft, is rejected while a below-cost line remains

Worked Example

A 10% promo pushes a $22 case under a $20 floor

Case Price (front-line)$22.00
Total Discount Percent10%
Net Case Price$22.00 × 0.90 = $19.80
Item Cost Floor (Last Landed Case Cost)$20.00
Below_Cost_Variance__c−$0.20 / case — Is_Below_Cost__c = true

In warn mode (the shipped default) the order still saves — the flag exists purely for pricing/finance to review later. In block mode, saving this line to a live invoice, or submitting a draft that contains it, is rejected until the price is fixed.

Two Modes, One Setting

Controlled by a single Custom Metadata record — Configuration_Preference__mdt.blockInvoiceOnBelowCost.

WARN — shipped default

Every below-cost line is flagged and reported. Nothing is blocked. Existing workflows don't change on day one.

BLOCK — opt-in after UAT

A below-cost line can't be committed to a live invoice, and a draft carrying one can't be submitted, until the price is corrected.

Where the Block Fires (once active)
✓

Commit to a live invoice

CreateInvoiceController, UpdateInvoiceController, Sell-From-Truck

✓

Draft submission

Draft → New; error names the offending item(s)

✓

Re-save of an already-flagged line

Runs on every update to a live line, not just price-changed ones — closes the gap where a line flagged in warn mode could dodge the block after it's turned on

◇

While a draft is being built

Flagged, not blocked — caught instead when the draft is submitted

Key Terms for Narration Consistency
  • Net case price — what the customer actually pays per case, after every discount is applied
  • Cost floor — the item's Last Landed Case Cost; the line under which a sale loses money on the case itself
  • Flag — the always-on, no-side-effect record of a below-cost line (Is_Below_Cost__c / Below_Cost_Variance__c)
  • Block — the opt-in enforcement that rejects a save/submit while a below-cost line exists
  • Pipeline-agnostic — the check works no matter which system set the price
