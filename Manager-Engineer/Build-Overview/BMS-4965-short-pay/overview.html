New here? Short-pay / AR primer

New to accounts-receivable and to Salesforce? Read this first. It explains, in plain language, every idea this epic touches. Everything below assumes you have read it.

What is a "short pay"?

A customer pays less than the invoice asks for. The unpaid slice is called a deduction. In beverage distribution this is routine — a case arrived damaged, a promised promo discount wasn't on the bill, or the customer disputes a line. The shortfall must be captured (why did they pay short?) and later resolved (do we forgive it or chase it?).

What is AR (Accounts Receivable)?

The money customers owe Gulf but haven't paid yet. When a customer short-pays, the leftover stays in AR as a balance owed. The AR/finance team's job is to keep that balance explained and shrinking.

What is an Invoice?

The bill for one delivery. In Salesforce it is the record Invoice__c. It already carries Total_Due__c (what's owed) and Amount_Paid__c (what was collected). When Amount_Paid__c < Total_Due__c, that gap is the short pay.

What is a Credit?

A credit memo / write-off — the record Credit__c. Posting a credit against the invoice (Credit__c.Invoice__c) is how Gulf says "we're forgiving this deduction." A posted credit is one of the two ways a short pay gets resolved (the other is the customer eventually paying — "collected").

The driver's role

The delivery driver is at the customer's door. If the customer short-pays, the driver picks the reason on the spot (e.g. "damaged goods"). That capture step already shipped under a sibling ticket, BMS-3844, in the driver's mobile app. This epic does not touch that — it builds everything that happens after the truck leaves.

Why "back office"?

"Back office" = the desk work after the delivery: someone in AR reviewing the open short-pays, escalating the big ones to compliance, and posting credits to close them. The driver captures; the back office resolves. This epic is the back office.

What is "escalation" + FL/AL compliance?

Past a dollar threshold or once it's aged too long, a short pay gets routed to compliance instead of being quietly written off. Gulf operates in Florida and Alabama, and beer-franchise / credit law differs by state — so the threshold rules are per-state and configurable. The exact FL vs AL numbers are still pending from Gulf.

What is a "review queue"?

A worklist of open short-pays for AR to work through — practically, a Salesforce list view (a saved, filtered table) over invoices that are still Open or Under Review, with a screen to follow later. It is not a new object; it's a view over the invoices that have an open short pay.

A worked example

A bar is invoiced $1,000 for a beer delivery. Three cases arrived broken, so the bar pays $880. The driver taps "Damaged Goods" as the reason. The $120 gap is the short pay:

Total_Due__c = $1,000.00   −   Amount_Paid__c = $880.00   =   $120.00 short pay
→ sits in AR as a balance owed, status Open → AR reviews it → posts a Credit__c for $120 → status Resolved-Credit.

Go deeper — the capture → escalate → resolve lifecycle

One short pay travels through these stages. Stage 1 already exists (shipped under BMS-3844); stages 2–5 are what this epic builds.

  1. Capture already shipped · 3844
    At the stop, the driver picks Invoice__c.Short_Pay_Reason__c (and optional Short_Pay_Note__c). In code, E_DriverHome sets these when the collected amount is short. Do not re-touch this path — duplicate writes would conflict.
  2. Land in the review queue
    The invoice gets Short_Pay_Status__c = Open. AR works a list view filtered to Open / Under Review. Short_Pay_Amount__c (= Total_Due__c − Amount_Paid__c) shows the dollar gap.
  3. Escalate (if it crosses a threshold)
    A B_ batch checks each open short pay against the per-state CMDT (Short_Pay_Threshold__mdt: state → $ + aging days). Over the line → Short_Pay_Status__c → Escalated, routed to FL/AL compliance.
  4. Resolve
    Either AR posts a Credit__c against the invoice (forgive the deduction → Resolved-Credit), or the customer eventually pays and AR marks it Resolved-Collected. Short_Pay_Resolved_Date__c stamps the close.
  5. Roll up & report
    The closed short pay feeds Account.Short_Pay_Count_TTM__c / Short_Pay_Amount_TTM__c so repeat offenders surface, and native Reports/Dashboards summarize by reason / status / date.
Go deeper — why reuse Invoice__c + Credit__c instead of building new objects

The original children of this epic assumed brand-new objects — Short_Pay__c, Escalation__c, Repeat_Offender__c, even an OHFY-Core package. None of those exist in the codebase. The decision was to reuse what's already shipped instead. Why:

  • Capture already lives on the invoice. The driver writes Short_Pay_Reason__c / Short_Pay_Note__c straight onto Invoice__c. A short pay is an attribute of one invoice — a separate object would just shadow data the invoice already holds.
  • The dollar gap is already derivable. Total_Due__c and Amount_Paid__c are on the invoice, so Short_Pay_Amount__c is a formula, not new stored data.
  • Resolution already has a home. Credit__c exists with an Invoice__c lookup (relationship Credits) and even a QuickBooks credit-memo sync. "Resolved by posting a credit" reuses Gulf's real write-off mechanism instead of inventing one.
  • Repeat-offender = a rollup, not an object. Counting short pays per customer is a TTM rollup onto the existing Account — a Repeat_Offender__c object would only duplicate it.
  • Fewer moving parts, no migration. Zero new objects means no data migration, no new sharing model, and the work is purely additive — new fields, one CMDT, one batch method, one permission set.

One collision to avoid: use a distinct Short_Pay_Status__c, not Is_Locked__c — the latter belongs to B_Invoice_LockInvoices (delivery-prep) and reusing it would clash.

Go deeper — why "drivers see capture only"

Drivers capture the reason but must not see the resolution status, the escalation state, or the AR balance. The principle is "capture, don't chase" — a driver's job is to record why the customer paid short, not to negotiate or collect the debt.

Mechanically this is enforced with Field-Level Security (FLS) via a permission set: the new Short_Pay_Status__c and AR fields are hidden from the driver profile, and driverHomePage never surfaces resolution state. The driver app stays exactly as it shipped under 3844.

🧱 What's being built

  • Invoice__c.Short_Pay_Amount__c — Currency/formula = Total_Due__c − Amount_Paid__c.
  • Invoice__c.Short_Pay_Status__c — Open / Under Review / Escalated / Resolved-Credit / Resolved-Collected / Written Off — + Short_Pay_Resolved_Date__c. This IS the review queue. Distinct from Is_Locked__c (collision risk).
  • Per-state escalation-threshold CMDT (Short_Pay_Threshold__mdt) — state → $ amount + aging days (placeholder values pending Gulf).
  • Escalation batch — a B_ batch flipping Short_Pay_Status__c → Escalated past threshold.
  • Credit__c-based resolution — Credit__c.Invoice__c; a posted credit closes the deduction.
  • Account repeat-offender rollups — Short_Pay_Count_TTM__c / Short_Pay_Amount_TTM__c (no Repeat_Offender__c object).
  • AR-suppression permission set — FLS hiding Short_Pay_Status__c + AR fields from the driver profile.
  • Native EOD reporting — Report / Dashboard on Invoice__c by reason / status / date.
